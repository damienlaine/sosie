# Welcome

[INTRO] Welcome to Sosie! This little demo project has five lines.

[MIC] Press Space to record this line, then Space again to stop.

[SYNTH] Don't feel like talking? Press S, and a synthetic voice will say this sentence for you.

# What next

[RYTHMO] In Rythmo mode the text scrolls by: say each word as it crosses the bar.

[END] The voice track is ready in the project's export folder. Your turn!
